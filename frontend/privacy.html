Privacy policy

What information this website uses, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: September 2026. PetCare Connect is an informational pet adoption and care website. This page describes the intended handling of information in this site; review it alongside the privacy terms and notices of any linked service before sharing personal information.

Information you provide

If you create an account, the site uses details such as your name, email address, and phone number to establish and manage your account. Adoption forms may also collect contact details and information about your household and experience so a shelter can review your application. Information entered into veterinary or boarding enquiry forms is placed in an email draft on your device; it is not submitted to this site.

How information is used

  • To authenticate your account and show your profile.
  • To manage saved pets and adoption applications.
  • To respond to messages you choose to send.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.

Storage and service providers

When the site is configured, account and adoption data may be processed by the Supabase project configured by the site operator. The site also uses third-party content and libraries, including hosting/CDN, web fonts, and map search links; those providers may receive standard technical data when your browser requests their services. Please consult their privacy information for details.

Browser storage

This site may use browser storage to maintain an authentication session and remember saved pet IDs or application details on your device. Other people with access to your device or browser profile may be able to see locally stored information. Sign out on shared devices and clear site data when appropriate.

Retention and your choices

Data stored in a configured account or database is retained according to that service’s configuration and the site operator’s practices. You can ask about access, correction, or deletion by contacting the site team. Deleting local browser data removes information stored on that device, but does not delete server-side records.

Children and sensitive information

This site is intended for adults planning pet care or adoption. Do not submit payment details, passwords in message forms, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email the PetCare Connect team or visit our contact page.

This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. The site operator should confirm that it accurately reflects the live deployment, data retention, and applicable legal requirements.
